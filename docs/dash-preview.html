How TripperDash++ looks on the dash

A to-scale mock-up built from the renderer code (MapViewSource.swift, docs/dash-visible-area.md). The map area is the 526×300 frame the app streams; the lower part and the turn card are drawn by the dash itself. Toggle the options to see each overlay.

Who draws what

  • App (in the video): map, route line, heading puck, battery + ETA boxes, speed-limit sign, weather pill, notice, zoom badge.
  • Dash itself: turn card (disc, bottom-left), incoming-call card, gold status band, speedometer, odometer.

Positions follow the code and the measured visible area (circle centre 262,263 r 264; turn card disc centre 79,228 r 68). The map tiles, glyphs and fonts here are stand-ins, not pixel-exact. Heading puck height and the zoom badge spot are approximate. Not a substitute for a photo of the real dash.
